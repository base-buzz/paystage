# PayStage brand kit — the venn

Generated by `node scripts/brand-kit.mjs`. Every value comes from `apps/console/src/lib/pass-tokens.json`, the same
file the wallet pass and the mandate page read, and the mark's numbers come from `scripts/lib/venn.mjs`, which the
pass renderer also draws. A pick here cannot drift from the product.

## The pick: the venn

**Chosen 2026-11-27: the venn** — two plates, 62 × 62, radius 15, at x = 0 and x = 38, overlap 24 units =
**38.7%**. The lens (the intersection) is the only differentiated element: gold active, red revoked, grey expired,
paper knockout for one colour. **Revised 2026-11-28 (#460): plate B is 62% of plate A over the surface** — the
original same-fill pair merged into one silhouette, visible only as a gold lens.

Why this one:

- **The state is the mark.** The overlap is the one part of the shape that changes, so active/revoked/expired read
  without a second drawing, a badge or a colour swap on the whole mark.
- **It survives the icon slot without a variant.** At 29 px the lens is ~6.96 px wide — above the 3 px floor. The
  old outlined second plate needed a small-size variant to survive 29 px; a mark with a variant is two marks.
- **It is not a letter.** The previous options included a P and a card; the venn reads as a mark at 16 px, not as
  "V", "B", "O" or "8".

The earlier three-option comparison is kept below as history, not as a live recommendation. The seal, the monogram
and the pass are **retired**; the seal's drawing function was deleted from `scripts/brand-kit.mjs` because nothing
references it any more.

## The comparison that led here (retired)

| option | idea | strengths | why it was not picked |
|---|---|---|---|
| **mono** | a **P** whose counter is a gold bar | legible down to 16 px, reads as a letter not a glyph, works on any background | says "P", not "gate"; a letter is not a product mark |
| **seal** | the **seal on the pass face** — a frame with a keyway and a dot | literally the product: the same shape a holder sees in Wallet | busiest at small sizes, and the keyway needed a compact variant below 48 px — two drawings |
| **pass** | a **card with a clipped corner** and a gold stripe | unmistakably a pass/ticket; distinctive silhouette | at 16 px it is a blob; horizontal, so it wastes a square icon |
| **venn** | **two solid overlapping plates**, state in the lens | one drawing for every state and size; the overlap is the message | the pair could read as a generic ven diagram without the lens-as-state rule |

The original recommendation was the monogram. It was a reasonable call for a favicon and the wrong one for a product
whose whole claim is a boundary that can be open, narrowed or revoked. The lens carries that claim in one colour.

## Type

One face: **Noto Sans**. The bundled copy in this container ships a **single weight**, so anything that asks for 700 is
drawn at regular weight plus a 2 % stroke to fake it. On the live site — where the font is loaded properly — use
**700/600/400** for real and delete the stroke. This is a rendering-container limit, not a brand decision.

## What changed on 2026-11-27

- `markVenn()` replaced the seal as the lead mark; the seal, monogram and pass functions were deleted.
- The mark is now rendered at **240 / 120 / 60 / 29 / 16 px**; the lens is **6.96 px at 29 px** (floor 3).
- Lockups are **vertical**, **horizontal** and **mono**; the old per-option lockups are gone.
- Favicons, the OG card, the pass face, the poster marks and every storyboard frame that bakes the mark were
  regenerated in the same commit.
- `scripts/check-brand-assets.mjs` now asserts the geometry, the 29 px lens and zero-byte/missing files, and runs in
  `ci-local`.

## To wire it (after a fork)

1. Set the chosen mark as `apps/console/src/app/icon.svg` and `favicon.ico`, and the same in `apps/vouchguard`.
2. Replace the header text in `apps/console/src/app/page.tsx` with `lockup-<pick>.svg`.
3. Point the OG route at `og-1200x630.png` and update `check-badges`/`check-titles` if they assert on the old text.
4. Keep this file's table as the history of the choice.

## What this is not

It is not a trademark clearance, and it is not a typeface licence — Noto Sans is OFL, which is fine for the web, but the
wordmark itself should eventually be drawn as outlines rather than set in a font, or a re-render on another machine
changes the letterforms.
