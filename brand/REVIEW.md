# PayStage brand kit — the ticket

Generated by `node scripts/brand-kit.mjs`. The mark's numbers come from `scripts/lib/mark.mjs`, which the wallet pass,
the verification page, the loop and every check also read. The text palette comes from
`apps/console/src/lib/pass-tokens.json`. A pick here cannot drift from the product.

## The mark: a cinema ticket with the Lucide print

**Settled 2026-11-27** (commit `14daf83`, `/work/ops/docs/proposals/mark-final-spec.md`): a **356 × 208** ticket, body
inset 5 with radius 28, **four concave corner scallops** (r = 0.085 H = 17.68) and **two larger mid-edge notches**
(r = 0.105 H = 21.84, one per end at `y = H/2`). Straight edges are smooth — no serration. The print is Lucide
`fingerprint-pattern`, all nine paths on a 24-grid at stroke 2, scaled to 58% of H (k ≈ 5.03).

Why this one:

- **It reads as a ticket, not a rounded rectangle.** The two mid notches are the tear and the four scallops are the lace
  edge; the notches are ~25% larger so they survive where the corners soften.
- **State is in the print.** One teal ticket, three inks: white active, `#B42318` revoked, `#8A8F98` expired, paper
  knockout for one-colour. The silhouette does not change, so the same drawing is used everywhere.
- **Three drawings, chosen by width.** Full ≥142 px; reduced 64–141 (the corners and both notches, print stroke
  thickened); icon <64 px is a square teal tile (radius 22%, white print at 56%). At 89 the corners soften, at 64 they
  are marginal and the notches still lead; below 64 the ticket is a sliver, so the tile takes over.

The print is **Lucide's** craft under **ISC**, not ours: keep its licence notice where the icon set is redistributed, and
do not treat a borrowed fingerprint as a PayStage trademark. The ticket is ours.

The pass assets (`apps/console/public/pass/`, the storyboard frames and the signed demo pass) are regenerated by

## Type

One face: **Noto Sans**. The bundled copy in this container ships a **single weight**, so anything that asks for 700 is
drawn at regular weight plus a 2 % stroke to fake it. On the live site — where the font is loaded properly — use
**700/600/400** for real and delete the stroke. This is a rendering-container limit, not a brand decision.

## History (retired)

The earlier marks — the monogram, the keyway seal, the two-plate venn, the two-bite ticket and the narrow-lens ticket —
are superseded. The venn's state-in-the-lens rule was the first that survived colour loss; the final spec replaced it
with state-in-the-print because the ticket and the Lucide print carry the product better. The old `mark-venn-*` assets
are not generated any more.

## What this is not

It is not a trademark clearance, and it is not a typeface licence — Noto Sans is OFL, which is fine for the web, but the
wordmark itself should eventually be drawn as outlines rather than set in a font, or a re-render on another machine
changes the letterforms.
