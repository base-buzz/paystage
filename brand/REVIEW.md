# PayStage brand kit — three options to pick from

Generated by `node scripts/brand-kit.mjs`. Every value comes from `apps/console/src/lib/pass-tokens.json`, the same
file the wallet pass and the mandate page read, so a pick here cannot drift from the product.

Look at `sheet-<option>.png` first: each one shows the mark at **256 · 96 · 48 · 32 · 16 px**, and the last three are the
sizes a favicon is actually seen at. A mark that works at 256 and turns to grey mush at 16 is not a logo.

## The three marks

| option | idea | strengths | risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **mono** | a **P** whose counter is a gold bar | legible down to 16 px, reads as a letter not a glyph, works on any background | says "P", not "gate" — the concept has to come from the copy |
| **seal** | the **seal on the pass face** — a frame with a keyway and a dot | literally the product: the same shape a holder sees in Wallet | busiest at small sizes; the dot disappears under ~24 px |
| **pass** | a **card with a clipped corner** and a gold stripe | unmistakably a pass/ticket; distinctive silhouette | at 16 px it is a blob; horizontal, so it wastes a square icon |

**My recommendation: mono.** It is the only one that survives the favicon, and the favicon is the size most people will
ever see. The seal is the better *product* mark — it is what is on the pass — and it can stay exactly there, on the pass,
while the company uses the P.

## Wordmarks

- `wordmark-solid.svg` — ink, with a gold full stop. The dot is the cheapest way to make a flat wordmark feel finished.
- `wordmark-split.svg` — **Pay** in ink, **Stage** in muted. Quieter, and it survives on a light background at small sizes.
- `wordmark-plain.svg` — one colour, for stamps, invoices and anything that cannot take two.

Lockups: `lockup-{mono,seal,pass}.png` — mark left, wordmark right, for a header or a press kit.

## Type

One face: **Noto Sans**. The bundled copy in this container ships a **single weight**, so anything that asks for 700 is
drawn at regular weight plus a 2 % stroke to fake it. On the live site — where the font is loaded properly — use
**700/600/400** for real and delete the stroke. This is a rendering-container limit, not a brand decision.

## What is here

```
mark-{mono,seal,pass}-{light,dark}.svg|.png   the mark, 512 px, on indigo and on ink
wordmark-{solid,split,plain}.svg              the wordmark alone
lockup-{mono,seal,pass}.svg|.png              mark + wordmark
favicon-{16,32,48,180,512}.png                currently rendered from the seal
og-1200x630.svg|.png                          the social card
sheet-{mono,seal,pass}.png                    the review sheets — look at these
```

**Favicons are currently the seal**, not the recommendation, because the pick is tomorrow. When you choose, one line in
`scripts/brand-kit.mjs` switches them (`FAVICON = "mono"`).

## To wire it (after the pick)

1. Set the chosen mark as `apps/console/src/app/icon.svg` and `favicon.ico`, and the same in `apps/vouchguard`.
2. Replace the header text in `apps/console/src/app/page.tsx` with `lockup-<pick>.svg`.
3. Point the OG route at `og-1200x630.png` and update `check-badges`/`check-titles` if they assert on the old text.
4. Delete the unused two options, keep this file's table as the history of the choice.

## What this is not

It is not a trademark clearance, and it is not a typeface licence — Noto Sans is OFL, which is fine for the web, but the
wordmark itself should eventually be drawn as outlines rather than set in a font, or any re-render on another machine
changes the letterforms.
