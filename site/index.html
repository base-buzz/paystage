Safety tools for AI agent payments

PayStage sits in front of an agent's wallet and answers one question before money moves: should this payment happen? Preflight checks return pay, review, or do_not_pay.

Policy engine

Roadmap

Declarative rules for spend limits, allowed payees, and allowed networks. One policy.json per agent.

Payee allowlist

Roadmap

Spend guardrails

Roadmap
